XtraPath Explore — The Educational Instagram & GitHub for Math, Physics & STEM Creators

XtraPath is the creator network combining the visual social discovery of Instagram, the code transparency and forking of GitHub, and the design flexibility of Figma & Canva for educational sciences:

  • Educational Instagram Feed: Browse, like, bookmark, and follow educators sharing interactive physics simulations, geometry puzzles, and animated math proofs.
  • Educational GitHub & 1-Click Remix: Every creation is 100% source-coded. Fork and remix any project into your private workspace with a single click.
  • Lineage History & Content Evolution Tree: Visual derivation tree showing the exact genealogy of each creation, who forked it, and how concepts evolved over time.
  • Semantic Content Versioning (v1.0, v2.0): Track revisions, write changelogs, and maintain historical versions for classroom continuity.
  • Educational Figma & Canva: Infinite collaborative canvas with geometric primitives and customizable STEM templates.
  • Educational Pinterest & Visual STEM Ideas: Thousands of interactive anchor charts, math visual aids, physics cheat sheet infographics, and geometry proofs with 100% open source code and 1-click execution instead of flat dead images.
