About XtraPath

Empowering the world's educators, authors, and visual storytellers with interactive computing, multi-engine graphics, and creator monetization.

The Vision

Traditional technical publishing has been trapped in static mediums: dead PDFs, flat textbooks, and one-way video lectures. At XtraPath, we believe mathematics, physics, and computer science should be alive, interactive, and directly runnable.

We built XtraPath as an all-in-one studio where anyone can write LaTeX books with instant print-ready formatting, simulate complex physics in the browser, animate 3D mathematical concepts, and monetize their expertise globally.

The 4 Creative Pillars

XtraBook

Write professional LaTeX books, format for Amazon KDP print, and sell digital copies with live PDF.js cross-device previews.

ResearchLab

Turn scientific hypotheses into runnable physics simulations with interactive sliders and peer consensus.

Studio Suite

12+ rendering engines including Manim, Anime.js, Two.js, Zdog 3D, TikZ, and 3D Cartoon MoCap animator.

XtraStore

Direct monetization for courses, asset packs, and protected source code algorithms with Stripe, PayPal & Razorpay.

Built for the Global Community

Whether you are a university professor creating interactive problem sets, a self-publishing author writing a calculus guidebook, or a student visualizing differential equations for the first time, XtraPath gives you the superpowers to create, remix, and share knowledge without limits.
